# Construction dependencies and rollback

Read [surface-led decisions](15-surface-led-construction.md) first. Order follows dependencies, not universal irreversible rules.

Record units, tolerances and scale sources. With no dimensions, a declared normalized model is acceptable. Alternate planning with trials. Preserve untrimmed parents and checkpoints. Parent-first/detail-later is a common dependency; groove/fillet/part-gap ordering depends on intersections and retained surfaces. Large-before-small fillets is a candidate, not a law. Separate parts can be built independently using shared assembly datums and clearances.

Control-row derivative rules require appropriate clamping, knots, weights and nondegenerate natural edges; other rows are not universally safe to move. Trim edges require separate analysis. EdgeSrf does not require four equal-degree curves. Exact compatibility operations need not change geometry; inspect interior shape nevertheless. Same height shapes is not a universal Blend repair.

Zebra and EMap remain useful on small surfaces with suitable display settings and views; cross-check sections and numerical evidence. CurvatureAnalysis alone proves neither shape accuracy nor G2. Open appearance surfaces and automotive panels may be valid deliverables; watertightness follows the delivery contract. Protect existing document objects and check state before replaying timed-out operations. Report shape, continuity, structure and engineering checks separately, including unverified items.
