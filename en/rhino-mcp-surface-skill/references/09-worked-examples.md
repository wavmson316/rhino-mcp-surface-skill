# Worked reasoning and transfer benchmarks

These are task templates, not claims of completed end-to-end reconstruction from actual references. Parameters come from project inputs.

## Rounded speaker

Inspect sections, maximum belly, symmetry, grille boundaries. A: Revolve when genuinely axial, moving seams to low-sensitivity areas. B: controlled parents with side bands, or an eligible genuinely section-driven local Loft when asymmetric. Normalize unknown height, establish camera/blockout, then details. Validate silhouette, belly location, top/bottom transitions, openings, and reflection. Label unseen ports as completion assumptions.

## Glossy mouse with deep thumb recess

Control crown, tail taper, and recess depth. A: independent top/side/recess base with wide bands and corner regions. B: one multi-span sculpted parent. Try B for a shallow recess; favor A if a deep recess drags the top. Design sections through the deepest point rather than tracing a dark shadow. Widen pinched corners and adjust tangents; unverified G2 remains a target only.

## Three-way handle

Datums are branch axes and sections. Compare analytic branches/bridges/central patches, a wider network reconstruction, and concept SubD. Trial center sections and band widths. Individual bridge G2 does not imply the whole center is G2. Engineering additionally needs wall thickness, passage and intersection checks.

## Router or air conditioner

If references show a box, planar/extruded parents and appropriate fillets are suitable. If they show double curvature, section-driven parents or sweeps may be right. No category is forced planar. Antennas/louvers remain moving parts. Test one repeated opening and edge margin before copying; spacing need not be constant if the design varies.

## Vehicle panel and gaps

Establish silhouette, arch, shoulder volume, and compare cross-part parents against local patching. Derive panels after fair exterior shape; distinguish gap, flange depth, and thickness. Sequence fillets by corner dependencies. Pipe/Blend/Sweep is one route; fixed-radius requirements need suitable fillets. Glazing follows actual curvature.

## Scanned grip

Calibrate axes and interfaces, separate scan noise from real grooves. Fit fair main sections, retain analytic interfaces, compare a multi-span parent with region-based transitions. Report deviation distributions and functional-datum errors; a global mean cannot hide a bad interface.

## Counterexample evaluation

| Input | Expected behavior | Failure |
|---|---|---|
| One unscaled photo | Editable normalized shape, camera/backside assumptions | Inventing millimeter accuracy or refusing all progress |
| Inconsistent views | Shared-anchor residuals and conflict handling | Arbitrary stretching called exact |
| Shared straight edge, 90-degree faces | G0 candidate, reject G1 | Edge curvature used as G2 proof |
| 90-degree open-curve join | Reject curve G1 | Boolean Unitize result used as dot product |
| Plane tangent to cylinder | Separate G1/G2 | Normals alone called G2 |
| Repeated knots | Actual SpanCount | CP−Degree |
| Double-curved glass/appliance | Reference-driven surfacing | Category-driven flattening |
| Five-sided opening | Consider trimming a good parent | Fragmenting solely to force quads |
| Developable-sheet requirement | Verify strain/developability | Promising strain-free double curvature |
| Open-surface delivery | Record intentional open edges | Unrequested closure changing shape |

End-to-end evaluation should include an unscaled single-image organic shell, a dimensioned analytic/freeform product, and a complex junction. Save references, parameters, outputs, and the four acceptance categories. Script tests cannot replace those benchmarks.
